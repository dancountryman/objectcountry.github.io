Ping Pong is a family hobby since I was a little kid.  I almost don't remember not playing.  I remember barely seeing over the table.  Most people played in their basements, but we had a spare bedroom upstairs with a homade table that my dad had made when he was a kid.  It's always been one of my favorite things to do, but I've always had a hard time finding people to play.

So one of the first things I bought for retirement was a metaquest and the game [Eleven]( https://www.meta.com/experiences/eleven-table-tennis/1995434190525828/?srsltid=AfmBOorCu9wYPhRpsCq62CJfiL3nWq9Rnb6cgDgHLt7eUBFsuJp7FdzM) .  It is a virtual ping pong game.  It feels like the real game and you can play people around the world.  I 3d printed a paddle to hold the controller to make it feel more like a paddle. [Sanlaki Meta Quest 3 Paddle](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6285735/files)

I'm usually playing once a day.  Let me know if you want to play.
