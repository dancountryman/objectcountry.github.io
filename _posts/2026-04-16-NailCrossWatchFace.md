NailCross Watch Face

This was a journey, but I finally got my watch face approved on ZeppOS.  It uses a one of my new favorite logs of the Nail Cross.  I love having my own designed watch face that shares my Christian faith.  It is fun sharing it with people.  It incorporates a nail cross with the full date, the battery percentage with a circle demonstrating the percentage, temp outside, heart beats, steps, miles, and kcal.

<img src="assets/img/nailCrosPreview390x450_en.png" width="390">


I used the editor on https://watchface.zepp.com/ .

After I first got it done, I thought it would be an easy submission, but I went through over 4 reviews.  They have a pretty rigorous review process to make sure you meet their design standards.  But you can use your own designed watchface before it is approved.

I hope you enjoy it.
